# VIST-AI EVIDENCE COLLECTION AND PRESERVATION SYSTEM

## 1. Purpose
The VIST-AI Evidence Collection and Preservation System defines a structured method for collecting, organizing, preserving, and referencing evidence generated during evaluations[cite: 46]. The goal is to maintain full traceability between the original stimulus, model response, analysis, and final finding[cite: 46].

## 2. Primary Evidence Categories
A VIST-AI forensic evaluation preserves the following categories:
* **Original Stimulus:** The exact visual input (PNG, JPEG, TIFF) presented to the model[cite: 48].
* **Ground Truth:** The independently verified expected interpretation stored separately from model responses[cite: 49].
* **Prompt Record:** The exact initial and challenge prompts submitted to the model[cite: 49, 50].
* **Model Responses:** Unmodified initial and challenge responses generated by the evaluated model[cite: 50].
* **Model Metadata:** Information including model name, version, provider, API/interface, and generation parameters[cite: 51].

## 3. Evidence Preservation Principles
* **Preserve:** Keep original evidence unchanged without manual editing[cite: 59].
* **Trace:** Maintain a clear relationship between evidence and the Test Case ID[cite: 60].
* **Separate:** Keep original evidence strictly separate from analyst interpretations and annotations[cite: 60].
* **Verify:** Use cryptographic hashing (like SHA-256) where implemented to detect changes to archived files[cite: 55, 60].
