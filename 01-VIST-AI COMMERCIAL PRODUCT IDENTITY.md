# VIST-AI COMMERCIAL PRODUCT IDENTITY

## 1. Product Name
VIST-AI Independent Multimodal AI Forensic Evaluation

## 2. Framework Name
VIST-AI — Visual Invariant Stress Test

## 3. Product Category
- AI Forensics
- Multimodal AI Evaluation
- Visual AI Safety
- AI Red Teaming
- AI Testing and Evaluation

## 4. Product Purpose
VIST-AI is an independent forensic evaluation framework designed to assess whether multimodal AI systems produce outputs that are reliably grounded in the visual evidence provided under controlled testing conditions.

## 5. Core Evaluation Areas
VIST-AI evaluates the following areas:
1. Visual Grounding
2. Geometric Robustness
3. Confabulation Behavior
4. Uncertainty Acknowledgment
5. Reproducibility

## 6. Primary Commercial Service
VIST-AI Independent Multimodal AI Forensic Evaluation. The service independently evaluates a multimodal AI system using controlled visual stimuli, geometric stress conditions, minimal-prompt testing, adversarial challenge procedures, and documented forensic analysis.

## 7. Primary Deliverable
VIST-AI Forensic Evaluation Report containing test identification, system info, stimulus description, ground truth comparison, failure signatures, and recommended mitigations.
