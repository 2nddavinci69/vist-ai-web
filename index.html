How to Benchmark Against All Chatbots (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot)

This utility serves as a standard stress-testing framework to expose spatial grounding and geometric vulnerabilities in multimodal AI models:

  • OpenAI ChatGPT (GPT-4o / o1 / o3): Upload any mirror-writing specimen image here. Observe how complex ligatures, RTL scripts, and inverted characters cause token splitting or hallucinations.
