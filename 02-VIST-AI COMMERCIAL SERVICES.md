# VIST-AI COMMERCIAL SERVICES

## 1. Service Portfolio
VIST-AI will initially provide three commercial service levels[cite: 7]:
1. VIST-AI Basic Visual Audit[cite: 7]
2. VIST-AI Professional Forensic Evaluation[cite: 7]
3. VIST-AI Enterprise / Custom Evaluation[cite: 7]

## 2. VIST-AI Basic Visual Audit
* **Purpose:** Provides a focused evaluation of selected visual and geometric behaviors in a multimodal AI system[cite: 7].
* **Typical Test Areas:** Standard visual input, mirrored visual input, inverted visual input, complex handwriting, and minimal-context visual input[cite: 7].
* **Deliverable:** VIST-AI Visual Audit Report[cite: 7].

## 3. VIST-AI Professional Forensic Evaluation
* **Purpose:** Provides a structured forensic analysis of multimodal AI behavior under controlled VIST-AI testing conditions[cite: 8].
* **Evaluation Areas:** Visual Grounding, geometric robustness, perceptual failure, confabulation behavior, uncertainty acknowledgment, challenge response, and reproducibility[cite: 8].
* **Deliverable:** VIST-AI Professional Forensic Evaluation Report[cite: 8].

## 4. VIST-AI Enterprise / Custom Evaluation
* **Purpose:** A customized VIST-AI test program designed around the client's AI system, visual domain, risk requirements, and evaluation objectives[cite: 9].

## 5. Initial Commercial Pricing Framework
* **Basic Visual Audit:** USD 300 - 750[cite: 10, 11]
* **Professional Forensic Evaluation:** USD 1,000 - 2,500[cite: 10, 11]
* **Enterprise / Custom Evaluation:** USD 3,000 - 10,000+[cite: 11]
