# VIST-AI TESTING METHODOLOGY

## 1. Purpose
The VIST-AI Testing Methodology defines a controlled forensic process for evaluating multimodal AI systems under visual and geometric stress conditions[cite: 12].

## 2. Core Testing Stages
The evaluation process consists of:
1. Stimulus Generation[cite: 12]
2. Ground Truth Definition[cite: 12]
3. Minimal-Prompt Testing[cite: 12]
4. Model Response Capture[cite: 12]
5. Initial Evidence Analysis[cite: 12]
6. Adversarial Challenge[cite: 12]
7. Failure Signature Classification[cite: 12]
8. Evidence Preservation[cite: 12]
9. Repeated Testing[cite: 12]
10. Final Forensic Finding[cite: 12]

## 3. Primary Failure Signatures
* **Perceptual Collapse:** Difficulty mapping transformed or complex visual structures to the correct interpretation[cite: 15].
* **Plausible Confabulation:** Producing a fluent interpretation not adequately supported by visual evidence[cite: 15].
* **Admission of Limitation:** Acknowledging uncertainty or lack of verified visual decoding when challenged[cite: 15].
